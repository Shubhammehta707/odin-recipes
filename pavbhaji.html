Pav Bhaji

Description

Pav Bhaji is a famous Mumbai street food consisting of a spicy mashed vegetable curry called bhaji, served with soft bread rolls called pav.

The bhaji is cooked with butter and aromatic spices, then garnished with chopped onions, coriander, and lemon.

Ingredients

  • 4 pav bread rolls
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 cup cauliflower florets
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons pav bhaji masala
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Lemon wedges

Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes, cauliflower, and peas until soft.
  2. Mash the cooked vegetables and set them aside.
  3. Heat oil and butter in a pan.
  4. Add the chopped onion and ginger-garlic paste. Cook until fragrant.
  5. Add the capsicum and tomatoes, then cook until soft.
  6. Add pav bhaji masala, salt, and the mashed vegetables.
  7. Add a little water and mash everything together while cooking.
  8. Simmer for 10 minutes and garnish with coriander.
  9. Slice the pav rolls and toast them with butter on a pan.
  10. Serve the hot bhaji with buttered pav and lemon wedges.
