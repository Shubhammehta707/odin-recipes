Pani Puri

Description

Pani Puri is a popular Indian street food made with crispy, hollow puris filled with potatoes, chickpeas, and tangy, spicy flavored water.

It is a delicious snack that combines crunchy textures with sweet, sour, and spicy flavors.

Ingredients

  • 12 hollow puris
  • 2 boiled potatoes
  • 1/2 cup boiled chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 tablespoon tamarind chutney
  • 1 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon chaat masala
  • Black salt and regular salt to taste
  • 3 cups cold water

Steps

  1. Mash the boiled potatoes and mix with the chickpeas.
  2. Blend the coriander, mint, and green chilies with a little water.
  3. Mix the green paste with cold water, cumin powder, chaat masala, and salt.
  4. Add tamarind chutney to the potato mixture if desired.
  5. Make a small hole in the top of each puri.
  6. Fill each puri with the potato and chickpea mixture.
  7. Pour the spicy water into each puri and serve immediately.
