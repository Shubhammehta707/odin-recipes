Vada Pav

Description

Vada Pav is a popular Maharashtrian street food often called the Indian burger. It consists of a spicy potato fritter served inside a soft bread roll.

The vada pav is traditionally served with garlic chutney, green chutney, and fried green chilies for extra flavor.

Ingredients

  • 4 pav bread rolls
  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 8 curry leaves
  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying
  • Green chutney and garlic chutney

Steps

  1. Boil, peel, and mash the potatoes.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan and add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, and turmeric.
  4. Mix in the mashed potatoes, salt, and coriander leaves.
  5. Allow the potato mixture to cool, then shape it into balls.
  6. Mix gram flour, chili powder, salt, and baking soda with enough water to make a thick batter.
  7. Dip each potato ball into the batter.
  8. Deep-fry until golden and crispy. Take care when handling hot oil.
  9. Slice each pav roll and spread green chutney or garlic chutney inside.
  10. Place a potato vada inside each roll and serve hot.
